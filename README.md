## Description
SharpPlay is a custom music player I built for people who value privacy, customizability, and data. All your data is local, far out of reach from the people who seek to sell it. Moreover, you can explore your usage statistics for individual songs and your overall listening habits, with the latter being displayed elegantly on a graph within the app.
